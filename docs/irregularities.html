IR-56-07: eight literal containment flags mean DUPLICATE/STOP; similarity diagnostics do not waive the rule

The lane protocol's literal test (“more than 70% of your dots fall within 3 px of one registry raster's dots → log it as a duplicate and stop”) fires on 8 sparse registry rasters (containment up to 0.9989). Investigated with the discriminating statistics (the sibling GEMSDOE51 gate's own): every one of them is a habitat/superset emission 12–38× larger than this lane's 6,504 dots (13GEMSDOE lattice / toporef / union-tips, 5GEMSDOE pindrop-v4 discovery/nodes, GEMSDOE50 topo-lineament-scatter, GEMSDOE23 arrangement-matched-habitat, GEMSDOE37 h6-physics-dotted-80k), and for every one of them the 3 px Jaccard is ≤ 0.0908 and the reverse containment is ≤ 0.0462 (globally; ≤ 0.023 among the flagged eight) — this lane's dot set is a small subset of their habitat, not a re-issue. Against every prior submission of comparable construction (the 37,654–44,090-dot h33 family, incl. the 0.2778-attributed file), containment is ≤ 0.334 and Jaccard ≤ 0.052. Spearman rank correlation is at most 0.0429 (dots) / 0.1086 (surface) against all 412 unique registry rasters — far below the 0.90 threshold. Those metrics help describe the overlap but do not alter the literal result: protocol verdict DUPLICATE/STOP; no slot cleared. The flags are logged with full numbers in evidence/corrections/registry_check.json. Jaccard, mass ratios and reverse containment are diagnostic only and cannot override the stop rule.

IR-56-020: Footprint-edge fake features in the shared transforms (scarp_step / hessian_line / gradient_magnitude / rank01)

The transforms zero-fill outside the footprint before differencing or smoothing. Measured on the real footprint: scarp_step on iso_grav_anom returns median 5.28 on the 23,600-cell rim band vs 0.47 in the interior (p99 29.6 vs 2.1), and 56.5% of the top-25,000 cells of the ranked field landed on that rim band -- i.e. the data edge itself was the strongest 'fault'. A second-order effect: rank01 pushed NaN-within-valid cells into the top histogram bin (rank ~1.0), so edge-masking a transform without fixing rank01 would have re-created the same artefact. This defect invalidated the first E1 run (evidence/holdout_discovery_v0_prefx.json): every physics arm scored below the chance control while emitting mostly on the rim.

Checked / why it matters: tests/test_transform_edges.py (6 tests); the rim-vs-interior measurement printed in this session's log; re-run receipt evidence/holdout_discovery_v1.json

Done: Fixed ONCE in the shared template src/gems56/transform.py (allowed by the protocol, no private fork): scarp_step now requires full two-sided + persistence support inside the footprint; hessian_line masks a 3-sigma edge band; gradient_magnitude masks cells at distance <2 px from the complement; rank01 excludes NaN-inside-valid from the ranking and keeps them NaN. Six regression tests added in tests/test_transform_edges.py (each fails pre-fix). E1 re-run with identical pre-registered design; the pre-fix receipt is preserved as evidence/holdout_discovery_v0_prefx.json. · source: tests/test_transform_edges.py (6 tests); the rim-vs-interior measurement printed in this session's log; re-run receipt evidence/holdout_discovery_v1.json

IR-56-023: lane-uniqueness gate, 3 px proximity clause

On the round-2 dots-phase gate (evidence/lane_gate_discovery_v2.json) the literal brief rule '>70% of my dots within 3 px of ONE registry raster's dots' fires against 22 informative (non-probe) priors, and the coverage-adjusted policy agrees (DUPLICATE/STOP both). The trigger is density-degenerate: for every one of the 22, near_frac is within +/-0.03 of that prior's own 3 px halo coverage of the eligible grid (e.g. 0.887 vs 0.861, 0.740 vs 0.759, 0.700 vs 0.735), i.e. the clause is satisfied by any dot set whatever, exactly as IR-56-006 showed the same clause is vacuous at 1-dot counts against near-universal priors. The agreement tests pass: max Spearman 0.043 on the dots and 0.193 on the surface vs the 0.90 bar, zero identical hashes, and the screen's reciprocal >0.70 count is 0.

Checked / why it matters: Read literally, a raster whose 3 px halo covers >=70% of the grid makes the '>70% of my dots near it' clause unfailable for ANY candidate, so a STOP verdict carries no information about lane drift at that density; the mirror-image defect (IR-56-006) makes the same clause unfailable in the other direction at tiny dot counts.

Done: Protocol executed as written: the verdict DUPLICATE/STOP is logged, nothing is promoted, and the candidate was already NEGATIVE on the holdout.
