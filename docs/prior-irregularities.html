IR-56-009A sign-asymmetric crest detector in the lane's own code (found by a test, fixed here) — Three defects were caught while writing contract tests against synthetic scarps of known position: (a) the PCA strike vector had an arbitrary sign, so neighbouring pixels of one trace could place corrected dots on opposite sides; (b) the second difference was taken at 0.25 px sample spacing on bilinearly interpolated data, which turns the interpolation's piecewise linearity into a comb of curvature spikes one cell apart, and the 'nearest crest' rule then selected a spike instead of the scarp; (c) the prominence test measured a crest against its own flanking concave lobe, which exists only on one side of the trace, so right-hand scarps were rejected and left-hand ones accepted. (b) and (c) both shrink the offset histogram toward zero -- i.e. they could manufacture exactly the negative verdict this lane reported.badFixed in src/gems56/corrections.py (lane code, not a vendored template module, so no shared-tool fork was created): strike canonicalisation, a one-cell curvature baseline with a widened sampling window, full-kernel-support requirement, and a relative-strength rule symmetric in the sign of the offset. All of E1, E1c, E2, E3 and the uniqueness screen were then re-run from scratch. The remaining known bias (+0.2 to +0.5 px, constant in offset, shape-dependent) is characterised in evidence/estimator_validation.json and is why no claim here rests on an absolute sub-pixel offset.tests/test_contracts.py (10 tests) + scripts/validate_estimator.pylink
