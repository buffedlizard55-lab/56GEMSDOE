What must pass before any future upload

  1. Scientific gate: a calibrated, segment-level displacement consistent across independent evidence, exceeding approximately two 100 m pixels. Under the current receipts, 0 of 21 corridors qualifies, so this gate fails.
  2. Holdout gate: validate the newly selected hypothesis using the shared pooled holdout, whole buffered segments, pixel-exact visible-fault masking, and leakage canaries. Label every value HOLDOUT-DTI with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and a paired spatial-block 95% CI. A catalogue hide-and-recover result is not evidence of organizer scoring or of new-fault discovery.
  3. Uniqueness gate: compare the pre-placement and final rasters against the registered corpus. Log and stop on a duplicate trigger; do not bypass an inconvenient result.
  4. Format gate: single-band float32; finite probabilities in [0,1] within eligible data bounds; EPSG:32611; 100 m resolution; exact dimensions, bounds, and transform; null/NaN outside. The PR #5/#6 mask-aware path passes locally, but the legacy all-finite path remains inconsistent; agree and regression-test the shared source before relying on a future build. Portal acceptance remains untested.
  5. Governance: check the participant data terms and competition rules, use a unique name and a note of at most 140 characters, and keep the weekly-slot selection separate. Do not use a slot for an unvalidated research artifact.
