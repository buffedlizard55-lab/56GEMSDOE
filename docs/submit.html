What the portal accepts

Quoted from the competition's submission form, and consistent with the format section on the problem page: "You can submit a single-band GeoTIFF (.tif) file, or a .zip file containing a single GeoTIFF, with your predictions. It must match the submission format's CRS, shape, and geotransform." plus an optional note "A short comment to help you or your team tell submissions apart later".

Checks this repository runs on every raster before it is offered

$ .venv/bin/python -c "from gems56 import gates; print(gates.format_report('docs/downloads/h56-magpack-37k-20261009.tif','data/grid/sample_submission.tif'))"

and again from disk at build time (this file's receipt, the secondary's). The checks are: single band; float32; shape 3730 × 3292; CRS EPSG:32611; geotransform identical to the sample submission; min/max inside [0,1]; zero NaN inside the footprint; no nodata tag; and the dot count and the minimum distance to the catalogue.

After uploading

  • Record the score against the sha256 on the card - that pairing is what turns a submission into data.
  • Nothing in this repository is an organizer-confirmed score; label any number you copy onward as USER-REPORTED until the receipt page says otherwise.
